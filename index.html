Publications

CoDance: Learning Reactive and Compliant Human-Humanoid Interaction from Video

Zhuoqun Chen, Shucheng Jia, Boyuan Chen

Under review at ICRA 2027

project page arXiv code

From a single video of two human dancers, a Unitree G1 humanoid learns partnered dancing: it follows a moving partner's footsteps while keeping two-hand contact and yielding compliantly to interaction forces, sustained over repeated forward and backward motion with a real person.
  • Built an asymmetric actor-critic PPO pipeline with a privileged critic and decoupled upper/lower-body rewards.
  • Designed domain randomization from measured sensing bias and communication latency.
  • Deployed the policy on Unitree G1 with real-time motion-capture observations.

Super-Ellipse Control Barrier Functions for Enhanced Obstacle Avoidance in Complex Multi-Agent Environments

Wenbin Liu, Shucheng Jia, Mikhail Svinin

IEEE ICRAS 2025

project page paper code

Safe multi-robot navigation with control barrier functions that use super-ellipse boundaries instead of circles, so robots can pass rectangular obstacles without overly conservative detours. I built the overhead-camera perception pipeline (OpenCV, pixel-to-world mapping) and the CBF-QP controller in ROS. In a dense multi-robot intersection, task completion time dropped from 123 s to 62 s versus standard CBFs, in Gazebo and on real robots.

Projects

Autonomous Dual-Arm Tic-Tac-Toe

Personal project, Duke · Fall 2025

project page

Two Kinova Gen3 Lite arms see the board with an RGB-D camera, choose their moves, and take turns picking and placing pieces. I built the full pipeline: RGB-D perception, rule-based game logic in Python, ROS 2 integration, and MoveIt 2 pick-and-place. About 90% task success in Gazebo and 50% on the real arms.

Pneumatic Soft-Finger Robotic HandTeam of 3

MEMS Capstone, Duke · 2025

capstone page

A three-finger silicone soft hand with closed-loop grasp-force control. I led the control design: a two-phase (approach / stabilize) dual-pump PWM controller on an Arduino Mega using fingertip force, bend and chamber-pressure feedback, with safety supervision for pressure limits and release.
